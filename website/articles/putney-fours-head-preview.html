Putney Fours Head: crews gear up for Thames showdown

More than 200 crews are entered for Saturday’s race over the 4.25-mile course.

By Jordan Ellery27 October 20262 min read

The Thames will be a hive of activity on Saturday when the Putney Fours Head gets under way, with over 200 crews from across the capital and beyond battling the tide and the autumn chill.

Favourites in the men’s senior category are the Hammersmith Reach Boat Club, who have won the event for three straight years and have added two recent national-team trialists to their line-up.

What to watch for

The women’s event looks wide open. Barnes Bridge Rowing Club have the strongest recent form, but a young Kew Reach crew posted the fastest time at last month’s head-to-head trial and will fancy their chances.

Conditions look challenging. A brisk south-westerly is forecast, which could create choppy water on the Surrey side of the river and favour crews with the nerve to steer a tight line.

Spectator guide

  • First crew away: 11:30am from Chiswick Eyot
  • Best viewing: Putney Embankment and Hammersmith Bridge
  • Entries: 214 crews, 1,700 rowers
  • Free entry for spectators

Race organiser Sophie Lindqvist said the sold-out entry list was “proof that the head-race tradition is alive and well on the Tideway”.
