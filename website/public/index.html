Familiar problems we're building Doramino to solve

  • Test plans sit in spreadsheets.
  • Evidence is scattered across emails and IT tickets.
  • Findings stay unresolved because ownership is unclear.

Your critical supplier becomes unavailable. What happens next?

Your team receives a simulated alert. You contact the provider, make decisions, and record the response as it happens. Doramino tracks what happened and assigns the improvements that come out of it.

Illustrative example

  • Provider acknowledgement exceeded your target.
  • Recovery evidence is missing.
  • Two corrective actions need an owner.

Three workflows, one recurring cycle

Doramino's three initial workflows
Workflow What the customer does What Doramino provides
Incident-response drills Involve staff and IT providers in a simulated incident Timed scenario updates, recorded acknowledgements and decisions, and a review of missed steps
Recovery checks Run an agreed recovery test through their IT team or provider Test instructions, evidence requests, actual recovery timings and comparison with the customer's targets
Remediation and retesting Assign and resolve issues discovered during testing Owners, deadlines, supporting evidence and a record of whether the subsequent test passed
